Step-by-Step GnuCash Import Guide

1 Export Square CSV

In Square Dashboard, go to Transactions → Export → Transactions CSV, or navigate to Balance → View all transfers to download settlement files.

2 Convert & Reconcile

Drop the raw file into this utility. Verify the T-account equation balances to Δ $0.00, then click Download GnuCash CSV in the bottom bar.

3 Import into GnuCash

In GnuCash, select File → Import → Import Transactions from CSV. Check "Multi-split", assign the column mappings, and apply.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why isolate a Square Clearing account instead of posting directly to Checking?

Square bundles several days of transactions into lump-sum bank deposits after subtracting processing fees. Posting gross transactions straight into checking makes bank statement reconciliation nearly impossible. Routing to Assets:Square Clearing isolates unsettled revenue until the lump transfer posts.

How are Square fees handled in double-entry bookkeeping?

Square fees are an immediate cost of doing business. By booking gross sales to Income:Sales and debiting fees to Expenses:Square Processing Fees, your true gross revenue and total merchant costs remain 100% accurate on your profit and loss statement.

Is my accounting data secure?

Yes. This application runs entirely client-side using HTML5 and JavaScript. No financial records, sales amounts, or customer data ever touch an external server.
