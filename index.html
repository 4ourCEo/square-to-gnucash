Step-by-Step GnuCash Import Guide

  1. Export your Square Data:

    In your Square Dashboard, go to Transactions → Export → Transactions CSV (or Balance → View all transfers → Export).

  2. Convert with this Tool:

    Drop your CSV file into the box above. Verify your account names match your GnuCash chart of accounts, then click Download GnuCash Multi-Split CSV.

  3. Open GnuCash CSV Import Assistant:

    In GnuCash, navigate to File → Import → Import Transactions from CSV and select the downloaded file.

  4. Configure Column Mapping:

    Ensure the "Multi-split" checkbox is checked in GnuCash. Set:

    • Date column → Date
    • Description column → Description
    • Account column → Account
    • Deposit column → Deposit
    • Withdrawal column → Withdrawal
  5. Reconcile Your Clearing Account:

    The net amounts land in Assets:Square Clearing. When the bank deposit clears your checking account, record a transfer from Assets:Square Clearing to Assets:Checking. The clearing balance reconciles to zero.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why use a Square Clearing account instead of posting directly to checking?

Square bundles several days of credit card sales into a single lump-sum bank deposit after subtracting fees. Posting sales directly to checking makes monthly bank statement reconciliation nearly impossible. Using a dedicated clearing account (Assets:Current Assets:Square Clearing) isolates pending funds until the bank transfer arrives.

How are Square processing fees treated in double-entry bookkeeping?

Square deducts processing fees before transferring funds. In GnuCash double-entry bookkeeping, the processing fee is recorded as an expense debit (Expenses:Bank Charges:Square Processing Fees). Gross sales are credited as income, ensuring your true gross revenue and processing costs are accurately reported.

Is my transaction data uploaded to an external server?

No. All parsing and conversion occurs 100% locally in your browser memory using JavaScript. No transaction, customer, or financial data ever leaves your device.
