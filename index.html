How Square Reconciles in GnuCash

A professional CPA-grade workflow to eliminate mystery discrepancies between Square deposits and your checking account.

1

Export Square CSV

In Square Dashboard, go to Transactions → Export → Transactions CSV, or navigate to Balance → View all transfers to download settlement files.

2

Parse & Inspect Batches

Drop the CSV into LedgerFlow. Review the statement batch shelf, verify the revenue distribution bar, and inspect the balanced debits and credits.

3

Import Multi-Split CSV

In GnuCash, choose File → Import → Import Transactions from CSV. Check "Multi-split", verify column assignments, and complete the import.

Why isolate a Square Clearing asset?

Square bundles several days of transactions into lump-sum bank deposits after subtracting processing fees. Posting gross transactions straight into checking makes bank statement reconciliation nearly impossible. Routing to Assets:Square Clearing isolates unsettled revenue until the lump transfer posts.

How are merchant processing fees isolated?

Square fees are an immediate cost of doing business. By booking gross sales to Income:Sales and debiting fees to Expenses:Square Processing Fees, your true gross revenue and total merchant costs remain 100% accurate on your profit and loss statement.

Is my transaction data private?

100% yes. LedgerFlow runs entirely inside your browser using HTML5 and JavaScript. No customer data, sales figures, or financial records ever leave your machine or hit an external server.
